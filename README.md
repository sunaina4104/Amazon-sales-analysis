## Amazon Sales Data Analysis
## Overview
This project analyzes Amazon sales data to extract meaningful business insights using Python. It focuses on understanding sales performance, customer behavior, product trends, and regional demand through data cleaning and visualization.

## Objectives
Analyze overall sales performance
Identify top-performing categories and sizes
Study customer purchasing patterns
Examine regional sales trends
Generate data-driven business recommendations

## Tools & Libraries
Python (Jupyter Notebook)
Pandas
NumPy
Matplotlib
Seaborn
Scikit-learn

## Key Analysis Performed
Data cleaning & preprocessing
Sales distribution analysis
Outlier detection using IQR method
Sales trend over time
Top-performing states analysis
Courier status distribution
Correlation heatmap

## Key Insights
Most sales fall between ₹500–₹700
Sales peak observed during mid-April to early May
Revenue concentrated in Telangana, Karnataka, and Maharashtra
Operational factors show weak correlation with sales amount

## Business Recommendations
Maintain inventory for high-demand categories and sizes
Focus marketing efforts in high-revenue regions
Improve pricing and product variety for growth
